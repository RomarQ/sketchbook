Bitcoin Batch Simulator

Fill a vault with outputs, burn withdrawals against it, set the fee rate, and step through the batches the cluster derives. The rules port a bridge's batch derivation, as its Rust implementation builds it.

Gateway and chain

A deposit under the minimum payout is written off, and it funds the fee budget when it holds at least 5,800 sats. A burn must be at least the minimum payout plus its deduction. "Blocks from anchor to batch" is how long a batch window stays open: 0 matches the devnet, and about 3 matches mainnet, where median time trails the clock by about an hour.

Fee rate

The median fee rate of every block from a height on. A batch pays rate_0: the highest median of the six blocks that end at its anchor. It confirms in the first block whose median is at or below its rate. Every 36 blocks after its anchor, a rescue replaces a batch still waiting, at the market rate, once the market is at least 1 sat/vB above it.

Vault outputs

A row adds "Count" outputs whose values step evenly from the first value to the last. Leave the last value empty for outputs of one value. A coinbase output is spendable from the anchor 99 blocks above its block.

Withdrawals

A row adds "Count" burns whose amounts step evenly from the first amount to the last. Each burn is submitted at a Bitcoin height: "Burns per block" of them at "Start at height", and the next ones "Blocks apart" later. A batch anchored at that height or later can pay them. The gateway gives indices in submission order. An empty deduction takes the default.

Every batch

What the simulator models

  • Heights stand in for time. A withdrawal is submitted at a Bitcoin height: its burn lands on Midnight while Bitcoin is at that height, and a batch anchored there or later can pay it. That height is the burn's anchor: the first block whose median time reaches the burn time less a 90-minute lag.
  • A batch window closes a fixed number of blocks after its anchor ("Blocks from anchor to batch"). The cluster derives a batch at each new tip, and only one batch is in flight at a time.
  • An attempt confirms in the first block after its broadcast whose median fee rate is at or below the attempt's rate. Real blocks also take transactions below their median, so this overstates how long a batch waits while fees rise. Pinning by a recipient's child transaction and reorganisations are out of scope.
  • Coins on Midnight are not tracked. Any burn the gateway admits is a withdrawal, whether or not a deposit backs it.
The rules
  • Eligible outputs: a selection spends outputs of at least 5,800 sats, and a coinbase output only once the anchor plus one is 100 blocks above its block.
  • Input selection: the smallest single output that funds the batch, else the largest outputs first until they fund it. Funding is the net amounts, the fee at 100 sat/vB, and 5,800 sats of change. A batch holds at most 10,000 vB, so 171 inputs with one payout.
  • Choosing withdrawals: withdrawals in index order; one that does not fit is skipped, and the next one is tried.
  • Building a batch: the budget must cover the chosen batch's fee at 100 sat/vB. At rate_0 of 10 sat/vB or less, a paying batch also adds the largest unselected outputs. A size-limited withdrawal, one no 171 outputs fund although the vault's value covers it, does the same at any rate, with no payouts when nothing else is chosen. A fill stops at 10,000 vB or before the budget falls under 1,000,000 sats plus the batch's fee at 100 sat/vB.
  • The fee budget: the budget is every donation and write-off of at least 5,800 sats, less the fee of every vault spend. Each anchor adds the deductions of settled burns and of the burns a batch at the anchor may pay.
  • Anchors: the base anchor is the later of the last vault spend and the height of the oldest unpaid burn. Anchors step by 3 blocks until one builds.
  • The market rate: the market rate at a height is the highest of the six block medians that end there, clamped to 1 to 100 sat/vB. A batch's first attempt pays the market rate at its anchor.
  • Rescues: attempt b is evaluated at the anchor plus 36 × b blocks, while the batch is unconfirmed. When the market there is at least 1 sat/vB above the attempt in flight, a rescue pays the market rate. It spends the same inputs and pays the same withdrawals; only its change is smaller. The change already holds the fee at 100 sat/vB, so the budget funds every rescue.
